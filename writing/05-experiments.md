## 5.1 Unified evaluation protocol

All quantitative evidence in this paper is produced by a single evaluation harness, so that every row of every table is comparable by construction. Fusion quality is measured on the full 300-image M3FD test split (`dataset/manifests/m3fd_test.csv`; M3FD from TarDAL [CVPR 2022]) by the unified evaluator `eval_metrics.py`, operating on the luminance (Y) channel of the fused image, and reporting six standard metrics: entropy (EN), spatial frequency (SF), average gradient (AG), mutual information (MI), the gradient-based quality index for fusion (QABF [Xydeas & Petrovic, 2000]), and the visual information fidelity for fusion (VIF [Sheikh & Bovik, 2006]). <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §1 unified evaluator eval_metrics.py, Y channel, M3FD full-300; 机制消融实验协议.md §5 metric set EN/SF/AG/MI/QABF(+VIF) --> The same evaluator, the same channel convention, and the same per-image CSV output schema are applied to all eight re-implemented baselines and to all three S4Fusion variants (official weights, same-budget retrain B0, and CGA), so no method benefits from an easier metric implementation. <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §1; code/results/baselines/<method>_m3fd.csv per-image CSVs -->

The eight baselines — MetaFusion (CVPR'23), CDDFuse (CVPR'23), EMMA (CVPR'24), DCEvo (CVPR'25), W-Mamba (ICME'25), Diff-IF (InfFus'24), FusionMamba (VI'24), and the official S4Fusion weights (TIP'25) — are all evaluated under this unified evaluation protocol. <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §1; SOTA_MAIN_TABLE.md method list --> <!-- A-4 (2026-09-14) provenance caveat: the protocol unifies *evaluation*, not training. Every baseline is scored from its authors' released checkpoint, produced under its own training recipe and data domain, and only the S4Fusion family is retrained in-house. Cross-method comparisons therefore carry a provenance caveat, and the FusionMamba row in particular is a domain-transfer observation rather than a like-for-like retrain. --> One protocol note is required for FusionMamba: its VMamba [arXiv 2401.10166] encoder–decoder requires input side lengths divisible by 32, so 17 of the 300 test images were inferred with replicate padding to a multiple of 32 and cropped back to the original size (`test_m3fd_pad32.py`); the remaining images were inferred at native resolution. <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §5 FusionMamba pad-32 note; logs/fusionmamba_m3fd_infer.log -->

For downstream detection, we reuse the DCEvo detection branch checkpoint (`DCEvo_detect_branch.pt`) with the official M3FD XML→YOLO ground-truth construction, run on the full 300-image test set at input size 640, with a detection counted at IoU ≥ 0.5. <!-- src: code/arb_detect_full300.py (reuses run_m3fd_detection_eval.py XML→YOLO GT, weights=DCEVO_WEIGHTS, imgsz=640); diag_conflict_detection.py IOU=0.5 --> The high-conflict object subset is defined per the protocol from image gradients: for each annotated object, the mean gradient conflict between the infrared and visible source patches is computed as min(g_ir, g_vi)·(1−cosθ)/2 over the object bounding box, and objects in the top tercile of this conflict score form the high-conflict subset. <!-- src: code/arb_full300_stats.py conflict definition and 66th-percentile split; [stats] objects=2583 high-conflict=878 --> The same detector, the same ground truth, and the same subset definition are applied to every variant under test.

Statistical testing follows the protocol (`机制消融实验协议.md` §5): per-image paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with the image as the pairing unit, Holm correction within each family of comparisons, and exact binomial (McNemar-style) tests on recovered/lost objects for the detection endpoints. <!-- src: 机制消融实验协议.md §5 统计协议; GATE1_FREEZE §2–§3 --> All hypotheses, primary endpoints, and gate thresholds were fixed before the corresponding experiments were run; negative outcomes are reported as fixed, without post-hoc adjustment.

## 5.4 Pre-registered hypothesis verdicts

This section reports the verdicts on the hypotheses, each against the judgment criterion fixed in the protocol before the experiment was run. <!-- src: 机制消融实验协议.md §1 hypotheses and §10 grade table; GATE1_FREEZE §3 -->

**H1 — feature-space B/C/Δ state modulation improves structural fusion — falsified.** Under the controlled same-budget ablation (same public S4Fusion starting weights, same budget, seed 42), the state-modulated variant S significantly *degraded* every structural metric relative to B0: SF −0.0190, AG −0.0126, QABF −0.0005, with Holm-corrected p = 2.90e-44, 8.13e-40, and 5.63e-27 respectively; the only significant gain was MI (+0.0213). <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 H1 证伪; eval_H1.log lines 621-636 (SF −0.0190 Holm 2.90e-44, AG −0.0126 Holm 8.13e-40, QABF −0.0005 Holm 5.63e-27, MI +0.0213 Holm 1.09e-17, "S>B0 AND Holm p<0.05 structural metrics: NONE") --> The protocol's conclusion rule — "no structural metric with S>B0 at Holm p<0.05" — was met by the negative direction: the bottleneck is not SSM state capacity, and the paper's narrative pivots to image-space arbitration on the strength of this falsification, per the recorded consequence of H1. <!-- src: 机制消融实验协议.md §1 H1 row 不通过的后果; eval_H1.log "structural metrics with S>B0 AND Holm p<0.05: NONE" -->

The two conventions agree in direction and disagree in verdict. Every seed's delta is positive under both, but the criterion is met on one of three seeds under the budget-matched baseline against three of three under the frozen one, and the protocol's aggregation rule requires at least two. The pooled across-seed test is reported because the protocol requires it: stratified by seed (the Mantel–Haenszel reduction for paired binary data) it gives 42 recovered against 18 lost, p = 2.67e-03, and Fisher's method over the three per-seed p-values gives 2.02e-02. A pooled test can be significant while per-seed replication fails, and the criterion is the per-seed count: we report both, and we do not substitute the weaker statistic for the criterion. <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1/§3.8; code/pool_test_b3.py -->

The identical s123/s3407 rows in the single-baseline convention are explained by the shared baseline rather than by duplicated outputs: against their own 32-epoch baselines the same two seeds give 13/7 and 11/7, i.e. they separate as soon as the base stops being common, consistent with the pixel-level product check (0 byte-identical and 0 pixel-identical images in a 60-image sample). <!-- src: B1_B2_EVIDENCE §2 note; GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1 -->

**Stricter gates (C1/C2/C3) — narrow-margin misses under the single-baseline convention, further misses under the matched one.** Beyond the primary criterion, the protocol fixed three stricter gates; none is passed in full under either convention. <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 更严预注册门; GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1 -->

- **gate-C1** (high-conflict recall Δ ≥ +0.02): single-baseline convention, all three seeds FAIL (+0.0194 / +0.0159 / +0.0159, i.e. 0.0006–0.0041 below the threshold); budget-matched convention, all three seeds FAIL (+0.0159 / +0.0068 / +0.0046). <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3; GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1 -->
- **gate-C2** (mAP50 Δ ≥ +0.01 vs. its baseline): single-baseline convention, all three seeds FAIL (s42 +0.0085 → 0.7282; s123 +0.0028 → 0.7225; s3407 +0.0035 → 0.7232, against B0cmp 0.7197); budget-matched convention, all three FAIL with smaller margins (s42 +0.0084 vs. B0_e32 0.7198; s123 +0.0012 vs. 0.7213; s3407 +0.0016 vs. 0.7216). <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 gate-C2; GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1 -->
- **gate-C3** (SF/AG relative non-inferiority within −1%): single-baseline convention, s123 PASS (SF −0.39%, AG −0.60%) and s3407 PASS (SF −0.21%, AG −0.36%), s42 the borderline exception (SF −0.85% passes, AG exactly at −1.00%); budget-matched convention, SF passes on all three seeds (−0.591% / −0.171% / +0.026%) but AG fails on two of three (−1.576% / −1.052% / −0.792%), so the gate passes on s3407 alone. <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 gate-C3 冻结口径; GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1 (budget-matched relative values recomputed from results/seeds/*.csv) -->

The verdict for the grade table is therefore: the primary H5 criterion is met on all three seeds under the single-baseline convention and on one of three under the budget-matched one, and the three stricter gates are met in full under neither. The paper's wording for the gains that do survive is fixed throughout as *"statistically significant but below the stricter pre-registered gate thresholds"*, and we never write that CGA "passed the gates." Where the two conventions disagree, this paper follows the budget-matched one, because it is the comparison in which method and baseline were given the same training budget; the recorded consequence of that choice is the conclusion drawn here — the gain holds in direction everywhere and in significance on one seed, and it is not a replicated effect. <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.8 判定; docs/route_B_outline_20260916.md §1 -->

**Two finer statistics for the detector endpoint (B-4).** The endpoint above is reported from aggregate scalars; two finer checks were run afterwards and cut in different directions. ①**Per-image AP with a bootstrap interval**: per-image AP is computable offline from the stored per-image predictions, so the mAP50 difference gets an interval instead of a point — resampling images (B=2000, fixed seed) gives CGA−baseline = **+0.0161**, **95% CI [+0.0065, +0.0270]**. The interval excludes zero ⇒ the difference is distinguishable from chance (stronger than the aggregate point estimate supported), **but it does not exclude values below the registered +0.01 gate ⇒ gate-C2 is not unambiguously met and we do not claim it is**. The statistic is **not** ultralytics' mAP50 (per-image AP averaged over images vs. per-class AP with 101-point interpolation; absolute levels 0.8048/0.7887 here vs. 0.7282/0.7197 there) — both reported side by side. ②**All-object paired test with no conflict filter**: across all **2583** annotated objects, CGA recovers **44** and loses **13** (exact binomial **p = 4.7e-05**; all-object recall **0.6384** vs 0.6264). This addresses the selection-bias objection directly, since the high-conflict subset is defined by the same quantity that triggers the mechanism. <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §4; code/tier1_stats_map50.py -->

## 5.5 Mechanism visualization

The mechanism-level evidence verifies that CGA acts as designed rather than remaining inert. The global gate scale, which is zero-initialized so that loading the official checkpoint reproduces the baseline exactly, trains to saturation: tanh(gate_scale) = 0.9998 on the CGA seed-42 checkpoint. <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §4; cga_vis/summary.csv tanh_gate_scale 0.999822484097722 (all six samples); cga_visualize.py checkpoint abl_CGA_str_s42 --> This reading rules out the self-falsifying scenario in which the gate stays at zero and the measured gains would have to come from some other source; the arbitration path is genuinely open.

At the pixel level, the intervention is selective rather than global.
