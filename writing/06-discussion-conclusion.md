## 6 Discussion

This section reads the evidence in three registers: what it supports, what it does not, and where the method sits. The paper's contribution is a verified mechanism with an honest evidence profile; conflating the registers would damage exactly that.

### 6.1 What the evidence supports

The question of this study was narrow by design: at pixels where the infrared and visible modalities disagree, does an explicit image-space per-pixel commitment toward the locally salient modality recover what blending loses? Three observations support an affirmative answer at the level of mechanism.

First, the learned gate is genuinely engaged rather than latent. The zero-initialized global gate scale saturates after training at tanh(gate_scale) = 0.9998 <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §4 -->, which excludes the self-falsification scenario in which the gate stays at zero and any measured difference originates elsewhere in the pipeline. Second, the intervention is selective rather than global: on the six highest-learning conflict samples, the mean absolute CGA–baseline difference lies in 0.0125–0.0170 with difference-map peaks up to 0.594 <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §4 -->, and the modifications concentrate in conflict regions — exactly where the design intends them to act. Third, the direction of effect is stable across seeds while the magnitude is not, and the two are reported separately. Under the single-baseline convention all six quality metrics are sign-consistent on 3/3 seeds (EN, MI, QABF, VIF positive; SF, AG negative) <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §2 -->, and the H5 primary criterion is met on all three seeds: high-conflict recall gains of +0.0194, +0.0159 and +0.0159 (exact binomial p = 4.88e-04, 4.34e-03 and 4.34e-03) with recovered-to-lost object ratios of 20:3, 18:4 and 18:4 <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 (H5) -->. Under the budget-matched convention the same three seeds give +0.0159, +0.0068 and +0.0046 with the criterion met on one seed of three, and SF relaxes from 0+/3− to 1+/2− <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1/§3.8; b3eval 2026-09-16 -->. Under the decision rule — p < 0.05 and recovered − lost ≥ 3 — the criterion is met on one seed under the fairer convention; seed-level significance tests were omitted by the pre-registration itself, since with n = 3 the minimum attainable p is 0.25 <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §2 -->. We therefore report the direction as stable and the replication as convention-dependent, which is what was measured. The direction was first indicated by a training-free pre-check, in which committing toward the max-response modality in conflict regions recovers objects that blending loses; the learned gate confirms it under the criterion (cf. Introduction).

The profile of the quality metrics is coherent with this mechanism. Pooled per-image mean differences are positive for MI, QABF, VIF and EN, each with 3+/0− seed-direction consistency <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §2 -->; in the main comparison CGA attains the best QABF of the table (0.5449) and statistically ties the official weights on VIF (−0.0006) <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §1 -->. Commitment at conflict pixels improves cross-modal information transfer and perceptual consistency rather than high-frequency gradient energy — the two structural metrics that pay for it are treated in §6.2. The F3 stress axis points the same way: cross-scale drift-AUC falls from 0.8088 to 0.7839 (p = 1.41e-05), with significantly lower drift on four of five resolutions <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §5 (F3) -->. Committed outputs remain more consistent under rescaling — supporting, though not by itself proving, the reading that arbitration rather than averaging carries structure across scales.

### 6.2 What the evidence does not support

The freeze is equally explicit about the boundaries, and we report them in the same voice as the gains.

First, and carrying the most weight under the budget-matched convention, the primary detection gain does not replicate there: the criterion is met on one seed of three, against three of three under the single-baseline convention, and the protocol's aggregation requires two <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1/§3.8; b3eval 2026-09-16 -->. Both counts and the pooled across-seed test are reported, and the pooled test does remain significant (42 recovered against 18 lost, p = 2.67e-03) — a pooled test can be significant while per-seed replication fails, and the criterion is the per-seed count <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1; code/pool_test_b3.py -->. Second, the property that motivates the mechanism does not survive its own controls: requirement R2 asks that commitment act only where the modalities conflict, and both endpoints say otherwise — an all-ones map raises high-conflict recall by +0.0524 (p = 1.1e-05) while a shuffled map changes nothing (−0.0011, p = 1.00) <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §2; b3eval B-1 2026-09-15 -->. Third, the magnitude of the gain that does appear is statistically significant but below the stricter pre-registered gate thresholds. Gate-C1 (high-conflict recall gain ≥ +0.02) fails on all three seeds under both conventions (+0.0194, +0.0159, +0.0159 under the single-baseline convention; +0.0159, +0.0068, +0.0046 budget-matched), and gate-C2 (mAP50 gain ≥ +0.01 over its baseline) fails likewise in both (+0.0085, +0.0028, +0.0035 against B0cmp 0.7197; +0.0084, +0.0012, +0.0016 against each seed's own B0_e32) <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 (C1/C2); GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1 -->. The wording is fixed at "below the stricter pre-registered gate thresholds"; at no point do we claim the stricter gates were passed.

Fourth, the structural cost is real, significant, and mechanism-inherent. Under the single-baseline convention SF and AG pool to −0.0713 and −0.1295 with 0+/3− seed-direction consistency (Holm-corrected p = 1.09e-122 and 4.35e-147) <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §2 -->; under the budget-matched convention they pool to −0.0366 and −0.0988 (Holm-corrected p = 7.95e-56 and 1.40e-142), with AG still 0+/3− and SF relaxing to 1+/2− <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1; seeds_table_e32.md -->, and RGB_SSIM (−0.0093) and Colorfulness (−0.0043) move in the same direction <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §5 (K6) -->. A mechanism-consistent reading is that committing to one modality removes the averaging that partially inflates gradient-energy metrics at boundaries; we report this as the expected price of arbitration, not training variance, while flagging the reading itself as interpretive. The non-inferiority gate C3 passes on two of three seeds under the single-baseline convention (s123: SF −0.39%, AG −0.60%; s3407: SF −0.21%, AG −0.36%) with s42 the borderline AG exception at exactly −1.00% <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 (C3) -->, and on one seed under the budget-matched one (SF passes on all three; AG fails on s42 and s123) <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1 -->.

Fifth, the evidence mainline is single-dataset: every number derives from M3FD <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §1–§5 -->, so no cross-dataset generalization claim is made. The reliability-map distillation arm (B2) remains a negative result — validation NaN from the first epoch, no best checkpoint ever saved <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §5 (B2) --> — reported as a failure record, not rehabilitated.

### 6.3 Positioning

The comparison with the closest conflict-aware methods is defined on mechanism axes, not on gain magnitude. ARC3Fusion [Sensors 2026] coordinates consensus–complementarity–conflict in feature space through per-position three-branch Softmax weights that sum to one, and GatedFusion-Net [Information Fusion 2026] learns per-pixel Sigmoid confidence masks applied additively; both are continuous, feature-space, soft coordinators <!-- positioning per writing/02-related-work.md §6 -->. CGA differs on both axes at once: image space rather than feature space, and a hard commitment rather than a soft weight, triggered only where gradient-orientation or polarity conflict is detected. This positional distinction is independent of the gain magnitude — a small gain weakens the practical-value claim, not the axis distinction — and it is what survives the grade verdict intact.

The commitment philosophy itself has a long lineage. The classical select/choose fusion rules [Burt & Kolczynski 1993; Zhang & Blum 1999; Piella 2003] formalized choosing the salient source where sources disagree, and the learning-based multi-focus decision-map line [e.g., Ma et al., Neurocomputing 2021] still makes per-pixel binary choices — but driven by defocus within same-modality pairs <!-- positioning per writing/02-related-work.md §6 -->. CGA inherits the commitment idea and moves it to cross-modal conflict: the trigger is inter-modal gradient conflict, the commitment is learned end-to-end, and it is applied through a zero-initialized gate that preserves official checkpoint compatibility at load time.

Finally, the positioning is deliberately that of a mechanism verification, not a comprehensive superiority claim. In the main table EMMA leads SF and AG, and Diff-IF leads MI <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §1 -->; CGA's leading metric is QABF, with VIF a statistical tie against the official weights. S4Fusion serves as the testbed because it is the representative open scan-and-blend selective state-space backbone whose public weights make the falsification controlled — not because it is assumed to be the strongest backbone <!-- consistent with writing/01-intro-mechanism.md (K7) -->.

## 7 Limitations

The following limitations are reported exactly as recorded; none is mitigated by post-hoc argument.

1. **Structural cost.** CGA trades high-frequency structure for arbitration: pooled SF/AG differences are −0.0713/−0.1295 with 0+/3− seed consistency <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §2 -->. The C3 non-inferiority gate passes on 2/3 seeds; s42 is the AG borderline exception (−1.00%, exactly at threshold) <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 (C3) -->.
2. **Narrow miss on the stricter gates.** C1 and C2 fail on all three seeds <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 (C1/C2) -->. The wording is fixed at "statistically significant but below the stricter pre-registered gate thresholds" and never claims the stricter gates were passed.
3. **B2 distillation failure and late-stage val NaN.** The reliability-map distillation arm never produced a usable checkpoint: validation NaN from epoch 1, NaN on all subsequent epochs, best never saved; only an unusable last checkpoint remains, and per pre-registration it was neither re-tuned nor re-run <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §5 (B2) -->. Separately, CGA-line seeds exhibit late-stage val NaN (s123 from epoch 12, s3407 from epoch 13 <!-- A-5 2026-09-14: corrected 14 -> 13; GATE1_AMEND_20260914.md §3 explicitly supersedes the GATE1_FREEZE §5 value -->; best checkpoints 0.3090/0.3063 saved before onset), while the B0-line seeds run without NaN in every configuration retrained for this study — 20 epochs in the original queue (s123, s3407) and 32 epochs in the budget-matched control (all three seeds, 96 epochs in total, zero NaN, so the frozen claim that the B0 family is NaN-free survives the longer schedule) <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §5 (NaN 现象); GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.5 -->. The seed-42 CGA run whose best checkpoint feeds the main table selected its best at epoch 26 — beyond the 20-epoch B0 schedule — so the training length differs across arms; adopters who retrain the recipe on their own data face an unresolved divergence risk, and the best-validation selection rule is the only safeguard in this round. A commit-loss divergence hypothesis is recorded and left for future work; this round does not act on it.
4. **Single-dataset mainline.** The evidence is M3FD-only; out-of-distribution datasets were not part of this freeze round, and no generalization claim is made. The downstream-detection endpoint additionally uses a single fixed detector (the DCEvo detection branch) across all arms; within-arm comparisons are internally valid, but the absolute recall levels are detector-specific. A residual alternative explanation is therefore not fully closed: commitment shifts local contrast at conflict pixels, and part of the high-conflict recall gain could, in principle, reflect alignment of those shifts with this detector's preferences rather than improved fusion fidelity. The all-object mAP50 gains (positive on all three seeds, outside the conflict-subset definition) and the mechanism-external gate criteria are the evidence against this reading, but a second detector would be needed to close it definitively.
5. **Per-pixel independent gating.** The commitment decision is made independently at each pixel from local gradient conflict, with no spatial regularization on the commitment field. The observed concentration of modifications in conflict regions is an empirical property of the trained gate <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §4 -->, not an architectural guarantee; spatially regularized commitment fields remain untested.
6. **Registration sensitivity.** The conflict signal κ is computed from aligned source pairs; a 1–2 pixel misalignment can turn a shared edge into an apparent gradient-orientation conflict and trigger commitment toward one modality. The experiments use the M3FD pairs, so no robustness claim is made for misaligned inputs, and deployments with residual registration error should verify alignment upstream of the gate.
7. **Budget asymmetry, now controlled — and its residual.** The CGA arm follows the 32-epoch extended schedule while the main-table B0 baseline follows the unified 20-epoch schedule; the main-table deltas therefore bundle the gate, the commit-weighted loss, the gate learning rate, and the extended schedule. This round closes the gap instead of deferring it: a 32-epoch B0 control was trained for all three seeds and the primary criterion re-tested against it, which is the budget-matched convention reported in §5.4, and the answer recorded there is that the criterion is met on one seed of three <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1/§3.8; b3eval 2026-09-16 -->. Two residuals remain and are not hidden. First, the control is itself budget-limited: its best-validation epoch is the final epoch in all three seeds, so the baseline had not converged at 32 epochs and the comparison answers "at equal budget" rather than "after convergence"; a longer baseline budget could only improve the baseline and would shrink these deltas further rather than restore them, so this risk runs against the paper, not in its favour <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.7 -->. Second, the two arms are separate training runs and this pipeline's training is not deterministic (next item), so a single-seed delta of a few thousandths carries run-to-run noise of the same order and must not be read as a mechanism effect.
8. **Train-to-train nondeterminism.** Two runs of the same arm, with the same seed, the same recipe and the same starting checkpoint, diverge: their validation losses agree to about 4e-06 for four epochs and then separate, reaching a gap of 0.036 (about 7% relative) by epoch 20 <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.4; logs/abl_B0_s42.log vs logs/seeds_queue.log -->. Two consequences follow. For the claims here, differences of that order between independently trained single seeds are not interpretable as mechanism effects, which is why the primary endpoint is a paired per-object test on matched inputs and why replication is counted per seed rather than inferred from a pooled statistic. For readers who retrain the recipe, the exact numbers reported here will not be reproduced by a re-run; the distribution will be.

## 8 Conclusion

This study set out to locate the residual failure of scan-and-blend selective state-space fusion at modal boundaries. The controlled falsification returned a clear negative: feature-space modulation of the state dynamics, motivated by state-capacity dilution, significantly degrades rather than improves the structural metrics SF, AG and QABF under a same-budget protocol fine-tuned from identical public weights <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §3 (H1) -->. The bottleneck of boundary fusion in this operator class is not the state; it is the absence of an arbitration decision where the modalities conflict.

We introduced Commitment-Gated Arbitration as the corresponding mechanism: an image-space, per-pixel commitment toward the locally salient modality, carried by a zero-initialized gate that preserves the official checkpoint exactly at load time and saturates when trained <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §4 -->. The mechanism is online and its effects point the same way everywhere; its benefit is not stable, and its trigger is not what produces it. Under the protocol's first base convention the primary criterion is met on all three seeds; under the budget-matched convention — the one that gives method and baseline the same training budget — it is met on one seed of three, with one of two effective independent readings, while the pooled across-seed test remains significant <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.1/§3.8; b3eval 2026-09-16 -->. The inference-time content controls add the sharper result, at both endpoints: commitment applied everywhere does at least as well, and destroying the conflict map's spatial arrangement changes nothing measurable <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §2, §2.5 -->. What the evidence supports is the commitment (R1, R3), not its conflict-triggered restriction (R2).

The evidence boundary is reported with the same discipline: the gains that do survive are statistically significant but below the stricter pre-registered gate thresholds; significant SF/AG structural costs accompany them; the mainline is single-dataset; and the distillation arm is a failure <!-- src: GATE1_FREEZE §2, §3, §5 -->. Two properties of the pipeline itself are disclosed as well, because they bound what any single number here can mean: the baseline had not converged at the matched budget, and two runs of one arm with one seed do not reproduce each other to better than a few thousandths of validation loss <!-- src: GATE1_UNFREEZE §3.4, §3.7 -->. Within these boundaries the contribution is not a performance claim but three things that remain true: a falsification of the diagnosis this literature's own reasoning implies, a mechanism that is implemented and verifiably online though not a reliable source of gain, and the protocol — criterion layering, both base conventions, content controls, replication counted per seed — that made both the falsification and the negative result interpretable rather than ambiguous.

Whether spatially regularized commitment fields, cross-dataset validation, or a stabilized commit loss extend these gains remains open; we list them as questions, not promises.
